## The Work

This is Brandon's masterpiece: literary genius and engineering genius combined. It explores the dual hedges against inflation (recorded history and archaeology) through the lens of foundational thinking, civilization collapse, and what survives.

Inspired by Isaac Asimov's *Foundation* series and the recursive methodologies that inform the Fairchild Grid chip design.
